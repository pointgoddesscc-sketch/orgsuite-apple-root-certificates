About certificates

Root Stores contain Root CA certificates preinstalled with Apple operating systems. They establish the validation chain used for HTTPS and other signed certificates. Configuration Profiles must not re-include those system roots.

OrgSuite policy

  • Use the platform Root Store on Apple devices.
  • Do not ship a parallel OrgSuite public-root bundle on iOS/macOS.
  • Do not store private keys in this repository.
  • Public sites must chain to a CA already in Apple's current list.
